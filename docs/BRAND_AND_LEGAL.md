# Brand and legal notes

This is a working checklist for the project, not legal advice. Have a lawyer review the name and the asset list before a public launch.

## Identity

- Product name: StoryMaker
- Mark: Lumen, a round lantern-fox drawn as original SVG in `apps/desktop/src/components/ui.tsx`
- Sample story: *Lantern and Tide* (Mira Quill, Bram Heddle, Sera Voss, Pio Marsh, the Quiet Pier). Do not replace this with text or names taken from another product's marketing or app.
- Theme names are original: Hearth Parchment, Moss and Lantern, Harbor Ink, Blossom Tea, Candlelit Study, Royal Plum.

## What we may echo

Feature ideas (a character sheet, a wiki, a timeline, a custom calendar, a writing desk) and a generally warm, bookish mood.

## What we do not copy

Logos, names, taglines, icons, illustrations, marketing sentences, sample lore, screenshots, exact palettes, or files from another product. Do not decompile another app to reuse its code, CSS, or assets.
